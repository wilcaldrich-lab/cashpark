Methodology

CashPark only publishes a rate after reading it on an official government or issuer page (or a free government API) in the same session the site is built/updated.

  • Brokerage sweeps: brokerage disclosure pages.
  • Money market funds: 7-day yields on Schwab Asset Management, Fidelity, and Vanguard fund pages.
  • T-bills: U.S. Treasury Daily Treasury Bill Rates XML (ROUND_B1_YIELD_* coupon-equivalent yield and ROUND_B1_CLOSE_* bank discount) plus TreasuryDirect auction investment rates. The scoreboard and calculator use the coupon-equivalent yield only. A bank discount is never labeled as that yield. The weekday workflow commits updates to main, which is what GitHub Pages publishes.
  • HYSA and no-penalty CDs: each bank’s own rate page or the bank’s own rate feed. FDIC national average stays as a benchmark. Conditional and promotional rates are labeled; unverifiable rows are omitted.
  • As-of + source URL required on every row. Unverified rows are omitted.

Yields are not APYs. Money market funds are not FDIC-insured. Education, not advice.

Not investment, tax, or legal advice. Rates change. Confirm on the linked source before acting. CashPark v1 — verified rates only; gaps listed on the scoreboard.
