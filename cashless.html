Why cashscoreboard.com is writing about this

Our job is to show where idle cash earns something and to link every number to an official source. Cash that earns nothing in a drawer and cash that earns 4% in an account are different tools, but you need both to work. Money in the bank only helps if you can reach it and spend it. This section keeps a plain, sourced record of how that is changing.

No predictions dressed up as facts, and no product pitches. If we cannot link a source, it does not go in. For how bills work on the page — serial numbers, counterfeits, a stash calculator, and official lifespans — see the Paper Cash section.
